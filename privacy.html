1. Overview

VClipz (“we,” “our,” or “the application”) is a desktop application for preparing short gaming video clips and, when authorized by the user, publishing videos to YouTube. VClipz is designed to process video files and application data locally on the user's Windows computer.

2. Information VClipz accesses

  • Video and media files: Files or source footage that you select or otherwise make available to VClipz for clipping and editing.
  • Google/YouTube account information: If you choose to connect YouTube, Google OAuth may provide authorization credentials and the permissions you approve. The application uses these permissions to perform the YouTube actions you request, such as uploading a video.
  • Application settings: Preferences and workflow information needed to operate the application.

VClipz does not ask for your Google password. Authentication is handled by Google's authorization flow.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate VClipz, process user-selected media, maintain application settings, and carry out YouTube actions explicitly requested by you. YouTube access is not used for advertising, selling personal information, or building profiles for unrelated purposes.

4. Local storage and disclosure

VClipz is intended to store application data and OAuth credentials locally on your device rather than on a VClipz-operated cloud server. VClipz does not intend to sell your personal information. Data may be transmitted directly to Google/YouTube when required to authenticate or perform an action you request, and may be handled by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Your use of Google APIs is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control Google's services.

5. YouTube API Services

When VClipz uses YouTube API Services, your use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can revoke VClipz's access through your Google Account permissions. VClipz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored files, settings, and authorization credentials remain on your device until you or the application delete them. You can disconnect the Google account by revoking access in your Google Account permissions. Removing the application may not automatically delete every file or credential stored separately in your Windows user folders; check the application's documentation and local storage locations.

7. Security

Because data is intended to remain on your computer, you are responsible for securing your device, Windows user account, and backups. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Do not share your device or local application data with people you do not trust.

8. Children's privacy

VClipz is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, contact us so we can review the issue.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VClipz changes. The revised policy will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
